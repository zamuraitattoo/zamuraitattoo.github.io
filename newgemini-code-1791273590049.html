Fort Kochi • Ex-Canada Tattooer

Zamurai Tattoo & Piercing was founded on the principles of fine art, individual anatomy adaptation, and uncompromising medical-grade safety.

Having spent 2 years tattooing in Canada across high-volume licensed studios, our resident artist brings North American sanitary practices, refined client care, and versatile tattooing expertise straight to Fort Kochi.

Whether working on ultra-delicate single-needle fine lines, cyber-sigilism ornamentals, custom cover-ups, or aseptic body piercings, every session is approached with a commitment to artistic longevity and clinical sterility.

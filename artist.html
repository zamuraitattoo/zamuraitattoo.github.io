Artist Profile & Official Portfolio

Stevin Pollayil Jestin

Founder · Tattoo & Piercing Artist

Born in Fort Kochi.
Honed in Calgary, Canada.

Stevin Pollayil Jestin is a creative and detail-oriented tattoo and piercing artist with over seven years of experience in custom tattoo artistry. Born on April 22, 1997, in Fort Kochi, Kerala, he earned a Bachelor’s degree in Commerce (Taxation) from Mahatma Gandhi University while developing his lifelong passion for drawing and realism.

After completing professional tattoo training in 2019, Stevin worked at Instyle Tattoo & Training Institute in Kerala (2020–2024). He completed Skillgap Training in Tattooing conducted by NSIM (2021), earned a Professional Piercing Certificate from Alienz Tattoo School (2023), and completed Bloodborne Pathogen (BBP) & Safety Training.

From 2024 to 2026, Stevin expanded his career internationally at Legacy Tattoo Inc. in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, refining his signature style in black realism, portraiture, stippling/dotwork, pepper shading, and freehand/Procreate digital design while maintaining strict Canadian hospital-grade sterilization standards.

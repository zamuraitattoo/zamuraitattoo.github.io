Journey & International Experience

Born on April 22, 1997, in Fort Kochi, Kerala, Stevin earned his Bachelor’s degree in Commerce (Taxation) from Mahatma Gandhi University while pursuing his lifelong passion for drawing and tattoo artistry. After completing professional tattoo training in 2019, he worked as a tattoo and piercing artist at Instyle Tattoo & Training Institute in Kerala (2020–2024).

He completed Skillgap Training in Tattooing conducted by NSIM (2021), earned a Professional Piercing Certificate from Alienz Tattoo School (2023), and completed Bloodborne Pathogen (BBP) & Safety Training. From 2024 to 2026, Stevin expanded his career internationally at Legacy Tattoo Inc. in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, honing advanced black realism and stippling techniques under strict Canadian studio sterilization protocols.

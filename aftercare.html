Good aftercare.

Tattoo

Keep it clean

Follow the cleaning and dressing instructions given at your appointment. Wash your hands before touching the area and avoid unnecessary friction.

Tattoo

Avoid picking

Do not scratch, pick or peel flaking skin. Avoid soaking the tattoo and follow your artist’s guidance about swimming, sun exposure and exercise.

Piercing

Leave jewellery in place

Do not rotate or remove jewellery during healing unless advised by a qualified professional. Avoid pressure and follow your piercing-specific cleaning instructions.

When to get help

Increasing redness, warmth, swelling, worsening pain, pus, fever or feeling unwell can require medical assessment. For a suspected piercing infection, seek prompt professional advice and do not remove jewellery without guidance from a qualified clinician.

Aftercare needs vary by tattoo, piercing location and individual healing. This page is general information, not a diagnosis or a substitute for medical care.

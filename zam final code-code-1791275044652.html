ABOUT ZAMURAI
Art, Made Personal.

Zamurai Tattoo & Piercing is an independent fine art studio in Fort Kochi, Kerala, founded by Stevin PJ and built on a devotion to technical perfection.

Having honed the craft across India and Canada, Stevin brings an international standard of tattooing to Fort Kochi — blending delicate needlework, custom artistry, and stringent aseptic studio protocols.

We specialize in Single-Needle Fine Line, Stipple Dotwork, Black & Grey Realism, Cyber-Sigilism, and professional Aseptic Body Piercing using titanium jewelry.

We believe a tattoo is a permanent extension of your identity. Every custom piece is thoughtfully designed around body flow, anatomical placement, and individual narrative — avoiding cookie-cutter templates.

Fine lines. Flawless shading. Zero compromises.

Our studio employs multi-enzymatic ultrasonic cleaning, electric autoclave sterilization, and hospital-grade surface disinfection to ensure the highest safety standard for every single client.
